    12 — Research

    Caravan-1: an open-weight
    trade model.

    Zhang Qian, M. Polo, I. Battuta, Gulnora (camel)

    Abstract

    We present Caravan-1, a distributed system for moving goods and ideas across Eurasia. The model has roughly 200 kg of weights per camel and runs fully offline. Trained for 1 600 years on real-world data — deserts, mountains, markets — it learned paper, numbers, music and religion on the job. We find that silk is all you need.
